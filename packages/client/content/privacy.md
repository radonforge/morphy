# Privacy notice

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Morphy is an early-alpha browser game. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no login. This notice explains what personal data the game handles and why, as required by articles 13 and 14 of the GDPR.

## Who is responsible

The controller is [[LEGAL_NAME]], VAT number [[VAT_NUMBER]], registered at [[REGISTERED_ADDRESS]]. You can reach us about anything in this notice at [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]].

## What we process and why

- **Device ID.** A random identifier your browser generates on first visit. It is kept in your browser's local storage and sent to our server so your lifetime stats can be tied to the same browser. It is not linked to your real identity.
- **Callsign.** The name you type in the menu. It is stored on the server together with your stats and shown to the other players in your room.
- **Stats.** Rounds played, wins, catches and survivals, counted per device ID.
- **Chat.** Messages are relayed to the other players in your room. We do not store or log them.
- **IP address.** Your connection passes through Cloudflare (a tunnel) and may appear in the web server's access logs. We use it only to deliver and secure the service.
- **Preferences.** Volume, graphics quality, mouse sensitivity, your last map and avatar. They stay in your browser and are never sent to us.

## Legal bases

- Running the game, your callsign and your stats: our legitimate interest in providing the game you asked to play (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- IP addresses in access logs: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working.
- Browser storage: strictly necessary to make the game work, so it needs no consent.

## Cookies, analytics and advertising

We set no cookies. We use no analytics and no advertising, and we share nothing with advertisers. The local storage entries described above are strictly necessary for the game to remember you and your settings.

## Who else handles your data

- **Cloudflare** carries traffic between your browser and our server.
- **Hetzner** (Germany) hosts the server and the stats database.

Both act as processors on our behalf.

## How long we keep it

Stats and callsign are kept for [[STATS_RETENTION]], or until you erase them. Chat is not kept. Access logs are kept only as long as the web server's log rotation holds them.

## Your rights

- **Erasure.** Open the Stats pane in the menu and press **Reset my stats**. This deletes your stats and callsign from the server. Your browser keeps its device ID and settings; clear your browser's site data to remove them too. You can also ask us by email at [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]]. Tell us your device ID, which you can read in your browser's local storage under `morphy:deviceId`, so we can find your row.
- **Other rights.** You can ask for access to your data, correction, restriction, portability, and object to processing, by email at [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]].
- **Complaints.** You can complain to the Italian data protection authority, the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it).
