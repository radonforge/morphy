## The official instance's legal pages

`packages/client/content/privacy.md` and `packages/client/content/terms.md` describe the official
instance and name Radon Forge as the controller of its data. If you deploy Morphy yourself, you
are the controller of your players' data: replace both pages with your own before you go live.
