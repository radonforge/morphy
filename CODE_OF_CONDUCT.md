# Code of Conduct

Morphy adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
for everyone taking part in the project: issues, pull requests, reviews and any other space the
project runs.

In short: be kind, assume good faith, criticise ideas and not people, and leave out harassment,
insults and sexualised language or imagery.

Report unacceptable behaviour to [privacy@radonforge.com](mailto:privacy@radonforge.com). Every
report is read by the maintainers and kept confidential. They may remove comments, code or
contributions that break this code, and warn, suspend or ban the people responsible.
